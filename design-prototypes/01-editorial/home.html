"I want North Bridge PCs to feel professional and trustworthy without pretending to be something it's not."

Hi, I'm Jacob Skrove — I started North Bridge PCs in May 2026. It began the way a lot of these things do: I built a PC for myself, enjoyed the process, and realized I could build more and sell them.

I care more about finding good value and matching a system to what someone actually needs than about pushing the most expensive or flashy parts I can find. If a $500 build gets you exactly what you need, that's what I'll recommend.

I'm still relatively new to this, and I'd rather be upfront about that than oversell my experience. What I can promise is that I'll treat your build like it's my own.
